name: Scientific evidence architecture
description: The species evidence mode uses a stable typed registry with conservative baseline profiles and coverage validation.
---

The Evidence & Research mode is a peer mode beside Life and Anatomy. Its profile contract keeps direct material, interpreted traits, confidence, uncertainty, sources, evolutionary context, genetic limits, research history, debates, and open questions separate from existing dinosaur display data.

**Why:** The archive must distinguish what is preserved from what is inferred without fabricating citations, genomes, or scientific certainty.

**How to apply:** Add verified research as source-linked profile entries or overrides; retain explicit limited, unknown, and source-review-pending states for unsupported claims.
