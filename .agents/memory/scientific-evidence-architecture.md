name: Scientific evidence architecture
description: The species evidence mode uses a stable typed registry with conservative baseline profiles and coverage validation.
---

The Evidence & Research mode is a peer mode beside Life and Anatomy, and Specimen Archive is a fourth peer mode. Evidence profiles keep direct material, interpreted traits, confidence, uncertainty, sources, evolutionary context, genetic limits, research history, debates, and open questions separate from existing dinosaur display data. Specimen records separately connect species, individual fossils, evidence IDs, paper IDs, and official source IDs; literature records are ready for reviewed metadata.

**Why:** The archive must distinguish what is preserved from what is inferred without fabricating citations, genomes, or scientific certainty.

**How to apply:** Add verified research as source-linked profile or specimen entries; retain explicit limited, unknown, partially verified, and source-review-pending states for unsupported claims.
