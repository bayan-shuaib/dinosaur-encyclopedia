MUSEUM EXHIBIT SYSTEM V2 — SCIENTIFIC RECONSTRUCTION OVERHAUL

The current exhibit system is functional but not yet museum-quality.

This update must transform exhibits from simple numerical calculators into interactive scientific reconstructions.

The objective is for visitors to understand:

• What scientists think
• Why scientists think it
• What evidence supports it
• How confident scientists are

Every exhibit should feel like a premium Natural History Museum installation.
