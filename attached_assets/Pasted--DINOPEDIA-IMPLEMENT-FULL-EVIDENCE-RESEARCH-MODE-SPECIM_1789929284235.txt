# 1. PROJECT CONTEXT

Dinopedia is a digital museum and scientific exploration platform focused on dinosaurs.

Its purpose is to help users understand:

- Extinct organisms
- Their anatomy and adaptations
- Ancient environments and ecosystems
- Fossil evidence
- Scientific interpretation
- Individual fossil specimens
- Scientific research and uncertainty
- What studying the past can teach us about biodiversity and conservation

Dinopedia must distinguish between:

- Directly preserved evidence
- Scientific inference
- Reconstruction
- Hypothesis
- Unknown information
